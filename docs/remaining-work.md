## Stack Intelligence for players — 24 November 2026

**Stack Intelligence for players and the redesigned scan page, 24 November 2026.** Players can ask Stack Intelligence questions before they join (on `/scan`) and while they wait or after their round (on `/ticket`). The player version is separate from the staff assistant and uses **no language model**: `backend/app/player_assistant.py` recognises English and Taglish questions with fixed patterns and answers from checked templates filled with live facts: the active rules, the line (people waiting, cabinet state, an estimate from the last ten rounds, 45 s to 5 min each, 90 s if none), today's best score and how many players reached the top (numbers only, no names), and, for a signed-in player, their own ticket and round (place in line, score, how many rounds today scored higher, the first level where they lost width). The scan page now shows the live line strip, makes the camera view itself tappable ("Tap to scan"; the "Use camera" button stays), and places the assistant below the scanner. The ticket page adds "While you wait" (and "How did that go?" after a round) with suggestions that follow the ticket state. Design canvas: Player Scan & Queue Assistant. ## Operator roles — 24 November 2026

**Operator and administrator roles, 24 November 2026.** Staff accounts now have enforced roles. An **operator** (a booth helper) runs the line: Overview, Queue (call next, skip, pause or open admissions, Player didn't come, re-entry QR), Sessions, Analytics, the Machine page (request a device stop, hold a round for review, release an unstarted no-show) and Stack Intelligence chat with their own private memory. Only an **administrator** can void or recover an interrupted round's score, use the development simulator, review practice questions or training examples, and manage staff accounts. Administrator accounts cannot be changed from the dashboard; the local administrator password stays in `backend/.admin-credentials.txt`. Creating, resetting, switching off and on are audited (`staff_created`, `staff_password_reset`, `staff_disabled`, `staff_enabled`) with the username only, never the password. ## Operator re-entry QR — 24 November 2026

**Operator re-entry QR, 24 November 2026.** A player who lost their ticket (new phone, cleared browser, or the launcher was restarted so the tunnel address changed and their cookie no longer applies) cannot register again with the same student ID. On the Queue page, **Player lost their ticket?** finds them by nickname or player code (no student ID is shown or searched) and **Re-entry QR** shows a one-time QR. The operator first checks that the person in front of them is that player. Scanning opens `/reenter`, restores their own ticket and place in line, and signs the old device out. The code works once, for 10 minutes; making a new one cancels the previous one. It is stored only as a hash, issuing it is audited (`player_reentry_issued`), and it travels in the URL fragment, so it is never sent to the server or tunnel logs as part of an address; the page removes it from the address bar at once. Open the dashboard through the public address when making the QR (the same rule as the registration QR). Migration `007_player_reentry.sql` adds the table. Checks: 3 backend tests, 4 component tests and a real-browser journey (lost phone, QR, restored ticket, old phone signed out, second scan refused). Still open: a run on real phones over the tunnel.
